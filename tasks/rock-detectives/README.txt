ROCK DETECTIVES — MARS SAMPLE LAB

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP completely.
2. Open index.html in a current desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The game works offline. No installation or local server is needed.
Keep the assets folder beside index.html, styles.css and game.js.

HOW TO PLAY
Investigate each equal-volume rock sample with the scale, microscope and
magnet tester. Compare the clues with the reference guide, then choose the
matching Basalt, Sandstone or Conglomerate bin.

You can drag the rock onto equipment and bins, or simply click/tap them.
Tab and Enter also work. Dr. Ian offers hints. There is no timer, and wrong
choices can be retried without losing the clues you have collected.

After each correct sort, the bin fills with matching rocks and a central
paper field-notes sheet opens. Read the microscope image and three facts,
then continue. The sheet accumulates all three discoveries. Reopen it using
the FIELD NOTES button or READ MY FIELD NOTES on the winning screen.
PLAY AGAIN clears the collection and starts a fresh shuffled round.

SCIENCE NOTES
The 100 cm3 sample masses (290/220/250 g) and clear/none/weak magnetic
responses are selected training examples, not measured Martian samples or
universal classification rules. The microscope textures are illustrations.

Science sources:
Basalt: https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70026725
Sandstone: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/martian-sandstone-target-windjana-before-and-after-brushing/
Conglomerate: https://science.nasa.gov/blog/persevering-across-the-upper-fan-in-search-of-record-keeping-rocks/
