OXYGEN RESCUE — FIND THE LEAK

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP completely.
2. Open index.html in a current desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The game works offline. No installation or local server is needed.
Keep the assets folder beside index.html, styles.css and game.js.

HOW TO PLAY
Move over the pipe joints and follow the stronger scanner signal.
Click a joint to inspect it. Find the leak, pick up the patch, and place it
on the glowing joint. Press the fitted metal plate to tighten its four bolts,
then test the repair to restore the cabin air.

You can drag the patch, or click/tap the patch and then the leak.
Tab and Enter also work. Sound is optional. There is no timer.

WEBSITE HOSTING
Upload index.html, styles.css, game.js and the assets folder to any static host.

The Pixelify Sans font license is included in assets/pixelify-license.txt.
