SOLAR SWEEP — CLEAR THE DUST

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP completely.
2. Open index.html in a current desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The game works offline. No installation or local server is needed.
Keep the assets folder beside index.html, styles.css and game.js.

HOW TO PLAY
Move the astronaut cleaning brush over the red dust. No clicking or holding
a mouse button is needed. On a phone or tablet, drag your finger.
Keyboard users can Tab to the panel and use the arrow keys.

The progress bar measures the dust removed. At completion the remaining tiny
flecks clear, the Powers restored winning screen appears, and Dr. Ian comes
back online in full colour with a congratulatory message.

Play Again restores the dust and puts Dr. Ian offline for the next round.
Sound is optional and there is no timer.

This package includes the latest 10% larger cleaning brush and footprint.

WEBSITE HOSTING
Upload index.html, styles.css, game.js and the assets folder to any static host.

The Pixelify Sans font license is included in assets/pixelify-license.txt.
